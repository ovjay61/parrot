## ✨ Overview

**Parrot** is a flagship native media player built for personal libraries. Deeply inspired by **iOS Liquid Glass** aesthetics and **120Hz physics-based spring motion**, it brings translucent frosted depth, dynamic ambient lighting, and fluid tactile responsiveness to Android.

Powered by dual **MPV + Media3 (ExoPlayer)** engines with full support for **Emby**, **Jellyfin**, and **fnOS**, Parrot combines multi-source ratings, cross-server aggregated search, translucent glass playback controls, built-in update checking, and VR playback optimization to turn your private library into a pocket cinema.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎨 iOS-Inspired Liquid Glass & Spring Motion

- **Dynamic Ambient Lighting & Glass Refraction**: Automatically samples dominant poster colors for adaptive frosted highlights and refined glass borders.
- **Seamless Shared-Element Transitions**: Tap any hero banner or library card for fluid spring-driven transitions into detail views and natural bounce-back navigation.
- **Morphing Liquid Glass Dock**: Smoothly collapses into compact floating glass capsules while scrolling through your library and media shelves.
- **Unified Portrait & Landscape Glass Controls**: Minimalist icon-only capsules and floating control dock with automatic collision-aware hiding for popups.

### 🎬 Dual-Core Playback Engine

- **MPV + Media3 (ExoPlayer)**: High-performance MPV engine is free by default, supporting hardware acceleration, HDR / Dolby Vision tone mapping, ASS styled subtitles, multi-audio tracks, and online subtitle switching.
- **Thoughtful Playback Gestures**: Long-press speed boost, double-tap seek, auto skip intro/outro, screen lock protection, and VR playback optimization.

### 🔎 Multi-Server & Smart Discovery

- **Multi-Protocol Support**: Native support for **Emby**, **Jellyfin**, and **fnOS**, with one-tap server switching and STRM direct-play optimization.
- **Cross-Server Aggregated & Fuzzy Search**: Search across all added servers simultaneously with typo-tolerant fuzzy matching and instant navigation.
- **Rich Metadata Exploration**: Unified TMDB / IMDb / Douban ratings, TMDB reviews, cast & crew, genre and studio collections, Trakt sync, and automatic GitHub release update checks.
